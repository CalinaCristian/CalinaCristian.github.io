An interactive résumé

Drive My CV

Get behind the wheel in Bucharest, survive the traffic, visit every office where Cristian worked, then fly to Seattle and drive to Bellevue.

Controls
Drag the left side to steer or walk, and the right side to look. Use the gas / brake pedals while driving. Tap Use to interact, 🚶 to get out or in, 🧭 for navigation, and ⏸ for fast travel, position reset and settings. On foot, tap Run or push the movement stick to its edge to run.
WS drive · AD steer
Space brake · H horn (you'll need it)
E enter office / refuel · M map & navigation · K time, weather & season · C look back · L headlights
hold F fast-travel to the next stop · R stuck? reset to the road
Mouse: click the view to look around (Esc frees the cursor) · Enter get out and walk (Shift run · Space jump) · P camera
Mind the speed limits, the cameras, the pedestrians and your fuel gauge.
Skip the game → classic CV

Built with Three.js, vanilla JS and real Bucharest geography. Traffic facts from the TomTom Traffic Index 2025.
