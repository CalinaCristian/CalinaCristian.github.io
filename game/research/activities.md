## Space Needle (seattle, spot `space-needle`)
- **Elevator ride**: Elevators lift visitors to the observation level about 520 ft up in 41 seconds. In 2025 the first of three new double-deck, floor-to-ceiling glass elevators went in; the other two follow in 2027 and 2028.
  - When: Open all year; on 2026-11-07 the official site showed Sa 9:30–20:30, Su 9:30–19:30, Mo–Th 11:00–19:00, Fr 10:30–19:30 (hours change by season). General admission $35–55.
  - In game: 41 s cut-scene in the glass car, the city dropping away; HUD shows the height rising to 158 m.
  - Achievement: `lm-needle-up` "520 feet in 41 seconds"
  - Sources (high): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Needle, https://www.spaceneedle.com/
- **The Loupe: revolving glass floor**: The lower level reopened in August 2018 as the Loupe, an indoor observation deck with the world's first revolving glass floor. It turns once every 45 minutes. The Oculus Stairs connect the two levels, and through the glass oculus at their foot you can watch the elevators moving.
  - When: Same hours. A Loupe Lounge (cocktails) runs seasonally, September to May.
  - In game: Stand on the glass and look straight down 500 ft; the floor carries you around (one full turn = 45 min, or time-lapsed). Spot the elevator through the Oculus.
  - Achievement: `lm-needle-loupe` "Stand on the Loupe"
  - Sources (high): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Needle, https://www.spaceneedle.com/
- **Open-air deck and Skyriser benches**: The upper observation level is open-air behind floor-to-ceiling glass panels (2018 Century Project). The "Skyriser" glass benches tilt outward toward the view: downtown, Elliott Bay, Mount Rainier, the Olympics and Cascades, Mount Baker.
  - When: Same hours.
  - In game: Lean back on a Skyriser bench (camera tilts outward); identify Rainier, Elliott Bay and downtown for a 360° checklist (Rainier only when the real weather is clear).
  - Achievement: `lm-needle-360` "Seen from the Needle"
  - Sources (high): https://www.spaceneedle.com/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Needle

## Chihuly Garden and Glass (seattle, spot `chihuly`)
- **Galleries, Glasshouse and Garden**: A self-paced visit through eight galleries, the Glasshouse (about 100 ft long suspended sculpture in reds, yellows and oranges) and the Garden. Typical visit 1–2 h. Live glassblowing demonstrations and an audio tour are included. Photos are encouraged (flash allowed); no touching, no selfie sticks or tripods.
  - When: On 2026-11-07: Sa 10–18, Su 10–17, Mo–F 11–17. Combo tickets with the Space Needle.
  - In game: Walk the galleries in order; in the Glasshouse look up as the sun moves through the suspended sculpture; watch a glassblowing demo loop.
  - Achievement: `lm-chihuly-glasshouse` "Under the Glasshouse"
  - Sources (high): https://www.chihulygardenandglass.com/visit, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chihuly_Garden_and_Glass

## Pike Place Market and the Gum Wall (seattle, spot `pike-place`)
- **Fish throwing at Pike Place Fish Market**: When a customer orders a fish, the fishmonger in orange overalls calls the order and the crew shouts it back. The fish is then thrown over the counter to be wrapped. Up to 10,000 visitors a day watch in summer. A monkfish is sometimes made to "snap" at customers with a hidden line.
  - When: All year during market hours (Pike Street × Pike Place).
  - In game: Order a salmon: the call-and-response chant plays, a fish flies over the counter and you catch it with a timed click (the fish is thrown to the wrapper, not into the crowd).
  - Achievement: `lm-pike-fish` "Catch!"
  - Sources (high): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pike_Place_Fish_Market, https://www.pikeplacefish.com/
- **Add gum to the Gum Wall**: Post Alley under the Market, by the Market Theater box office. Visitors stick used gum on the brick wall: as of 2024 it was 54 ft long and 8 ft high, about 180 pieces per brick. Fully steam-cleaned in November 2015, September 2018 and November 2024; gum returns at once.
  - When: All year (open alley).
  - In game: Chew, choose a colour, press a blob on the wall; bonus for making a heart.
  - Achievement: `lm-gum-wall` "Part of the wall"
  - Sources (high): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gum_Wall
- **Rachel the pig**: A 550 lb bronze piggy bank by Georgia Gerber (1986); donations fund the Market's social services.
  - When: All year.
  - In game: Drop a coin; the plaque thanks you.
  - Achievement: `lm-pike-rachel` "Fed Rachel"
  - Sources (medium): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rachel_the_Pig

## Seattle Great Wheel (Pier 57) (seattle, spot `waterfront`)
- **Ride the Great Wheel**: A 175 ft wheel with 42 climate-controlled gondolas (up to 8 people each). A ride is about 12 minutes, three revolutions, reaching out 40 ft over Elliott Bay. More than 500,000 LEDs; light shows usually Fri–Sun evenings.
  - When: On 2026-11-07 the official site listed 11:00–21:00 daily (varies); New Year's Eve midnight ride by reservation.
  - In game: Board a gondola, three turns with free-look; the photo at the top faces the skyline and the bay.
  - Achievement: `lm-great-wheel` "Three times round"
  - Sources (high): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Great_Wheel, https://seattlegreatwheel.com/, https://seattlegreatwheel.com/faq/

## Pioneer Square (Underground Tour, totem pole, Smith Tower) (seattle, spot `pioneer-square`)
- **Bill Speidel's Underground Tour**: A 75-minute guided walking tour starting beneath Doc Maynard's Public House (614 1st Ave, Pioneer Place). It goes through the buried sidewalks and storefronts left when streets were raised after the 1889 fire, lit from above by amethyst vault-light prisms.
  - When: Every day, all year, on the hour 10:00–18:00; extra half-hourly tours June–September. Closed Thanksgiving and Christmas Day.
  - In game: Short scripted descent under the sidewalk; look up at the purple prisms from below.
  - Achievement: `lm-underground` "Below Seattle"
  - Sources (high): https://www.undergroundtour.com/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle_Underground
- **Read the totem pole**: The 1940 Tlingit replica of the Chief-of-All-Women pole (see pioneer-totem.md). Visitors read the figures top to bottom.
  - When: All year.
  - In game: Plaque with the figure order and the carvers' credit; looking up the pole highlights each figure in turn (no climbing, no touching).
  - Achievement: `lm-totem-read` "Read the pole"
  - Sources (high): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pioneer_Square_totem_pole

## Fremont Troll (seattle, spot `fremont-troll`)
- **Climb on the Troll**: The 18 ft concrete troll under the north end of the Aurora Bridge (Badanes, Martin, Walter, Whitehead, 1990) clutches a real VW Beetle with a California plate. Climbing on it for photos is the classic visit (it even appears that way in the game Life is Strange).
  - When: Open public space, all year; Troll's birthday party around Halloween (unveiled Halloween 1990).
  - In game: Climb hand → wrist → head with scripted holds; sit on the Beetle for a photo.
  - Achievement: `lm-troll-climb` "On the Troll's head"
  - Sources (medium): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fremont_Troll

## Hiram M. Chittenden (Ballard) Locks (seattle, spot `ballard-locks`)
- **Watch a lockage**: Two locks: small 30 × 150 ft, large 80 × 825 ft. Boats rise or fall between Salmon Bay and Puget Sound. They carry more boat traffic than any other U.S. lock; more than a million visitors a year.
  - When: All year; the large lock is drained for maintenance about two weeks in November, the small lock in March.
  - In game: Stand on the lock wall while the chamber fills; boats float up past you (watching, not operating).
  - Achievement: `lm-locks-lockage` "Locked through"
  - Sources (high): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiram_M._Chittenden_Locks
- **Salmon in the fish ladder**: The fish ladder (rebuilt 1976, 21 weirs) is part of the locks; an underwater viewing room shows migrating salmon. Peak sockeye July to mid-August; Chinook and coho later in summer and autumn.
  - When: Seasonal for fish; grounds and the Carl S. English Jr. Botanical Garden all year.
  - In game: Count salmon through the viewing window (only July–October in game time).
  - Achievement: `lm-locks-salmon` "Salmon spotted"
  - Sources (high): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiram_M._Chittenden_Locks

## Gas Works Park (seattle, spot `gas-works`)
- **Fly a kite on Kite Hill**: Artificial kite-flying hill with a sculptured sundial at the summit; views of Lake Union and downtown.
  - When: Daily 6:00–22:00. Climbing the plant towers is dangerous: several falls, three deaths since 2008. Entering the lake is prohibited (contaminated sediment).
  - In game: Kite minigame with wind from the live weather; stand on the sundial to read the time. No tower climbing, no swimming.
  - Achievement: `lm-gasworks-kite` "Kite on Kite Hill"
  - Sources (high): https://www.seattle.gov/parks/allparks/gas-works-park, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas_Works_Park

## Kerry Park (seattle, spot `kerry-park`)
- **Sunset postcard view**: Viewpoint on Queen Anne Hill with the most iconic skyline view: Space Needle in the centre, Elliott Bay, and Mount Rainier behind on clear days. Photographers line the wall at sunset. Doris Totten Chase's 15 ft steel sculpture "Changing Form" (1971) frames the view.
  - When: Daily 6:00–22:00.
  - In game: Postcard challenge: Needle plus Rainier in one frame (only possible when the real weather is clear); frame the skyline through Changing Form.
  - Achievement: `lm-kerry-mountain` "The Mountain is out"
  - Sources (high): https://www.seattle.gov/parks/allparks/kerry-park, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerry_Park_(Seattle)

  - When: Concert season roughly autumn to spring plus the festival; fge.org.ro did not respond (2026-11-07) for the schedule.
  - In game: Take a seat in the round hall during a rehearsal; the camera pans along the fresco from Trajan to 1918.
  - Achievement: `lm-ateneu-concert` "Under the dome"
  - Sources (high): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Athenaeum
