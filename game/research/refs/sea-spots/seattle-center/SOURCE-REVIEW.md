## Concrete corrections

- **Source-located public arrival and lift target.** OSM footway `w1136496123` runs `(0.2,27.2) → (0,24.9) → (−0.2,22.6) → (−0.7,18.9)`. The scene now arrives at its public approach and offers the abbreviated lift at its mapped endpoint between the south leg pair. Neither attraction nor source building moved. The former fountain arrival remains available on foot to the south. The exact ticket/security/elevator interior is not surveyed or claimed.
- **Height-bounded visitor barriers.** The Loupe/deck barriers supplied endpoint heights only. `OnFootController` understood those, while the local walker/strict route planner needed `y0/y1`. Consequently, the elevated concentric rings blocked the ground-floor approach. The same walls now carry both representations. The ground lift has an explicit ground height and condition; upper-floor controls stay conditional on their own floor.
- **Actual city view from the visitor ring.** Opaque cylinders in the lathe profile sat immediately behind opaque window meshes. The visitor version removes those duplicate window-fill strips and uses transparent glazing. That glazing no longer emits a beige fill at night or casts an opaque shadow. This opt-in affects the Seattle Center hero only; other scenes' distant Needle treatment is unchanged. Real floors, rims, core, mullions, barrier and roof remain.
- **Geometry budget.** All 2,600 far trees, 382 near broad trees, 53 conifers and 1,300 suspended glass forms remain. The original far-ground mesh contained 199,800 triangles. The scene opts into removal of seabed wholly below opaque bay/lake water, and smooth-patch triangulation at the same 24 m source grid. Coasts and steep transitions retain original detail; neighbouring patches share original-height edge vertices. This is error-controlled simplification, not lossless geometry. Defaults for other scenes stay unchanged. Independent final-mesh comparison must remain below 0.03 m; measured results are in the handoff evidence.
- **Guideway joins and honest cadence.** Consecutive monorail spans no longer overlap by 5 cm. Their end normals now meet at the same edge. The wait text describes the accelerated scene loop instead of implying a real ten-minute wait.
- **Thin art-glass rendering.** The 1,300 open Persian bowls retain the same vertices, triangles, colour, opacity and two visible faces. They use one double-sided draw instead of a backface submission for the entire sculpture followed by a frontface submission. This saves 169,000 submitted triangles when the sculpture is in view. It changes blend order, not geometry, and is not pixel-identical: inside-Glasshouse day/night A/B mean channel differences are at most 0.60/255, with 4.61%/3.29% of pixels changing by more than 5/255 in any channel. Both garden comparisons were pixel-identical. The local day/night review found every form and overlap retained; the forms themselves remain source estimates.

## Measured blocker checks

The original scene had 1,113,342 triangles. It now has **995,902**, with 199,800 → 82,552 far-ground triangles and 192 duplicate opaque window-fill triangles removed. The independent final-ground comparison used 186,276 off-grid surface samples; maximum measured old/new height difference was **0.0244156 m**. Another 113,424 sample locations lie in omitted seabed below opaque water. This is a measured error bound, not a proof of pixel-identical terrain or surveyed ground accuracy.

The original strict ground route to the elevator was unreachable. Its tour consequently selected a 175.9 m hero/plaque/photo route with no activity. The new strict route is 9.06 m, with all segments clear, and the actual on-foot controller covered it in 5.05 simulated seconds. The optional tour begins at the real elevator action and totals 42.00 m. Ground controls are enabled at the door; upper-floor controls are disabled there, and the ground control is disabled on The Loupe.

Actual controller checks cover the mapped approach, ascent with hurry, Loupe walk, look-down action, stairs, a full deck circuit, an outward barrier rejection, downstairs and the return elevator. All passed in clear day, clear night and day rain, with no JavaScript page errors. The existing tour regression passed 58/58 checks with no runtime errors. High quality, full postprocessing and 4096 shadow maps remained enabled. A wide 82° south camera rendered 1,536,185 triangles before the thin-glass pass correction and 1,367,185 afterwards; the final source recheck including the glazing-shadow correction rendered **1,367,089 triangles / 310 calls**. The above-context camera measured 375 calls / 1,295,753 triangles. Other final camera/condition observations and reproducible drivers are retained locally in `/workspace/reality-pass/seattle-center-fidelity`.

The first cold render is **503 calls / 1,392,434 triangles**, before settling to 310 calls on both following frames. Its environment/shadow warm-up therefore still exceeds the 400-call target. This branch does not claim that the cold-frame draw-call gate, target-device FPS or universal load-time gate passes.

Broader console collection also found a browser-default `/favicon.ico` 404 from the repo-root static server: the actual asset is `/public/favicon.ico`, and the dev bench has no icon declaration. This is distinct from a JavaScript page error and is neither hidden nor worked around by this scene branch.

These focused checks do **not** constitute a clean scene audit. Existing audit findings remain: two bad-geometry findings (the garden sun's zero normal and another degenerate mesh), about 40–42 depth-fight samples, one remote phantom collider and three below-floor crowd samples. No source-QA or READY gate is waived.

## Material gaps retained for source QA

- **Needle proportions.** The tower silhouette, leg/core/rim dimensions and base-pavilion profile remain the existing hand-model estimates. The public-footway and glazing corrections do not validate every structural proportion against measured plans or establish all-side photographic fidelity.
- **Glasshouse placement/height.** OSM `w329416207` is a roughly 14.4 × 31.4 m rotated footprint (about 18° off the scene axes); the current hand model uses its 23.5 × 34.4 m axis-aligned bounding box. Raw OSM also tags `height=10` and `roof:height=10`, while the official description says 40 ft / 12.2 m. This branch does not silently resolve that source conflict or rebuild the pavilion. The current floor/crowd relationship still produces three `pedBelow` audit samples.
- **MoPOP.** The current separated, rounded SDF masses differ materially from the photographed connected folded complex. The surveyed volumes, roof profile and all sides of its entrance/monorail passage have not been reconciled against sources. A footprint collider does not establish that the SDF precisely follows the outline. Do not call it fully faithful.
- **Garden/art forms.** The Persian profiles, hanging form count/distribution, beds, garden glass and shrub placement remain procedural estimates. The actual colour/photo puzzle and a glassblowing demonstration are not implemented. The current E action aims upward and awards the existing activity.
- **Surroundings/views.** Far midrises, hills, ground colour, shoreline simplification and tree distribution are illustrative estimates in the existing backdrop, not a building-by-building surveyed Seattle model. The recovered 360° view does not establish skyline fidelity.
- **Source coverage.** Local game views cover south/north/east/west/above, the visitor deck, Chihuly and MoPOP; the fresh reference set is partial and is not a matched camera/season survey of every side. Full source A/B approval belongs to the orchestrator. No READY promotion is justified by these fixes alone.
- **Performance.** Scene geometry now meets 1 M. Local Chromium/SwiftShader observations are not target-device FPS or a universal <3 s load guarantee. Warm-up PMREM/shadow work is reported separately from settled frames. Remaining source/load/audit gaps are explicit in the handoff.
