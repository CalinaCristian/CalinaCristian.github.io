# Table (Romanian backgammon) at "La Șahiști", Cișmigiu

Research for the seated game against Doru (`game/src/spots/backgammon-game.js`, rules in `backgammon-engine.js`,
his lines in `backgammon-banter.js`). Web research, 2026-11-11. Confidence is marked High / Medium / Low; "not
verified" means no source was found. Doru and the onlooker are fictional.

## Rules as played in Romania

- Two dice, thirty "puluri" (fifteen each), the standard backgammon starting position. High.
  [ro.wikipedia: Table](https://ro.wikipedia.org/wiki/Table)
- Opening: each player throws one die, the higher starts, a tie is thrown again. Romanian press guides then have the
  winner throw both dice for the first move
  ([ProTV](https://stirileprotv.ro/divers/cum-se-joaca-table-regulament-si-secrete-pentru-unul-dintre-cele-mai-populare-jocuri-in-romania.html),
  [Fanatik](https://www.fanatik.ro/cum-se-joaca-table-acestea-sunt-regulile-jocului-si-asa-se-aranjeaza-piesele-21018787));
  pages translated from international rules let the winner play the two opening dice. Medium. **In the game: the
  winner throws both dice again.**
- No doubling cube in traditional table; the federation president calls the cube an American addition. High.
  [Dilema Veche, interview with Liviu Radu](https://dilemaveche.ro/sectiune/tema-saptamanii/articol/si-la-table-sintem-in-urma-interviu-cu-liviu-radu-presedintele-federatiei-romane-de-table)
- Scoring: "linie" = a plain win, 1 point. "Marț" = 2 lines, when the loser has borne off no checker (from Turkish
  "mars"; Caragiale: "Pot face marț pe un profesor de universitate"). High. [dexonline: marț](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/marț)
- "Marț tehnic" = 3 lines, but sources disagree on what it is: the international backgammon (loser still on the bar
  or in the winner's home; ProTV, Fanatik), or a Romanian invention with six made home points. Medium / Low. **In
  the game: the international definition.**
- Match length: "la trei linii" is the most cited. Medium-High. **In the game: Doru keeps the score to three lines
  across games; each game stands alone for the achievement.**
- No touch-move: a move may be tried and taken back (Liviu Radu interview). High. **In the game: Take back.**
- Both dice must be played when possible, otherwise the higher: stated in Romanian guides (translated rules). Medium.
- Not verified for classic table: restrictions on hitting in your own home board.

| poartă | a made point (two or more checkers); "a avea poartă-n casă" = to be unassailable | High |
| pul descoperit | a blot | High (Dilema Veche) |

| zar, a da cu zarul, dublă | die / roll, to roll, a double | High ([dexonline: zar](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/zar)) |

| gherlă | a useless roll | High ([dexonline: gherlă](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/gherlă)) |
| chibiț, tablagiu | onlooker who comments, table player | High |

Dice names. Romanian dictionaries attest only a few of the old Turkish names: **dușeș** (6-6; also "a stroke of
luck", [dexonline](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/dușeș)), **epiec** (1-1, from "hepyek",
[dexonline](https://dexonline.ro/definitie/epiec)), and **șeș-beș** (in dictionaries as an old name of the game, so
its use for 6-5 is Medium). No Romanian source lists all 21 rolls; literature and reportage show plain Romanian
numbers ("patru-doi", "dă patru-patru"). "Dubeș" is glossed inconsistently and is not used. So Doru calls rolls in
Romanian and keeps "dușeș", "șeș-beș" and "epiec" as flourishes.

## Lines

Attested (used as they are, or with a word changed to fit the speaker):

- "Șase-șase, poartă-n casă." ([Ficțiunea](https://fictiunea.ro/2022/130/art8/), [JurnalMM](https://jurnalmm.ro/o-inventie-pe-zi-jocul-de-table/))
- "Te-am făcut, frate!" (Ficțiunea)
- "Joci ca la Dîlga, da' ai zar!", "Atacă-l acum, la baionetă!" (an onlooker), "Hai, că-ți trece timpul.",
  "Dau tare, să vezi și să auzi bine ce joc.", "Ești la mutare!", "Să fii sănătos." (to the loser): all heard in
  Cișmigiu, [Dilema Veche, "În Cișmigiu"](https://dilemaveche.ro/sectiune/tema-saptamanii/in-cismigiu-590567.html)
- "Mai fierbe zarurile alea cu busuioc.", "noroc porcesc"
  ([Dilema Veche](https://dilemaveche.ro/sectiune/tema-saptamanii/sahul-e-pentru-elfi-tablele-sint-pentru-gnomi-590568.html))
- "a face marț", "a rămâne marț" (dexonline); "zarul contează", playing "la risc" (Liviu Radu interview)
- "Lasă-l bă, nu-l mai tachina, că-i mic", "Am venit să joc, nu să mă uit la alții cum joacă!"
  ([Europa FM, learning table in Cișmigiu](https://www.europafm.ro/experiment-cum-am-invatat-sa-joc-table-in-parcul-cismigiu/))
- "Caietul tablelor din Cișmigiu" (the score notebook), a stake of one ban a game, a wristwatch on the table
  (Dilema Veche, "În Cișmigiu")

Not verified, and so not put in Doru's mouth as a proverb: "Zar ai, joc n-ai", "Zarul e orb". (The player's own
line "Zarul contează, nea Doru!" uses the attested phrase.)

Every other line in `backgammon-banter.js` is written for the game in the same register (polite "mata", "tinere",
the words in the table above) and is not a quotation.

## The place

- "La Șahiști" is the board-game corner in the west of Cișmigiu: old stone tables, chess, rummy and table, circles of
  onlookers. High. (Europa FM; [amfostacolo.ro](https://amfostacolo.ro/impresii9.php?iid=74499&d=parcuri-bucurestene--bucuresti))
- Only men, many caps; regulars arrive around nine, wipe the tables and set up; play is loud, risky and meant as a
  show for the onlookers; "zarurile zăngăne … piesele păcăne, lemn pe lemn".
  ([Dilema Veche](https://dilemaveche.ro/sectiune/tema-saptamanii/strategia-il-face-pe-om-590561.html))
- Not verified: stakes of a beer or a coffee; where the boards are made.
