`research.open_canopy` enables only an explicitly reviewed structural record. It requires nonempty `evidence` and numeric `topM > clearanceM > 0`; it never follows automatically from `amenity=fuel` or an OSM layer. Optional `estimateSupports` / `supportWidthM` requests clearly estimated support geometry. The builder emits `buildingCanopies[index]` with the full evidence, `classificationSource`, top/clearance/support provenance and actual output dimensions. Positive OSM height/minimum tags take priority. The original `buildingIds` identity and full footprint remain intact. `buildingSourceIssues[index]` retains invalid nonpositive heights rather than labelling the fallback a valid measurement.

The source roof remains an elevated colliding slab; its underside is visible, its top receives weather tint, and estimated support squares have physical colliders. Every support's whole footprint must be inside the roof, outside source holes, off all road ribbons and clear of other buildings. A reviewed open canopy's ground service lane may pass underneath the declared roof band. The fuel POI reads that source building and omits the separate guessed 10×16m canopy / pump / pylon template. The source location stays unchanged. This code does not modify road geometry or renderer culling.
