# Regional source terrain candidate

`seattle-regional-source.json.gz` is a separate numeric source, not a replacement for close-up spot/district data. It retains the original USGS Float32 samples, returned PixelIsArea centres/steps and source hashes. NHD features retain permanent/object IDs, raw attributes, spatial reference and complete original ring order. `waterCoordinates` encodes original Float64 bits by axis-wise XOR and byte-plane transposition; `ringRefs` indexes this lossless stream. The source contains 3,977 features and 1,053,918 points. Ninety selected lake/pond geometries are unavailable. No missing geometry is evidence of dry land. Raw GeoTIFF/API inputs remain external; source URLs, hashes and failures remain in the numeric ledger.

Local OSM geometry, literal height/level tags and authored street datums retain priority. Regional NHD additions inside the old terrain apron are restricted to outside the original detailed OSM coverage, exclude every existing water polygon, and never replace its level. All 5,808 mapped context trees are inside that detailed coverage. The original directed OSM coastline closes the reviewed canonical ±6,000/±5,400 m frame and retains marine-mask priority there, including its land side; it is incomplete at 7 km. Marine NHD supplies the outside region. The earlier hypothesis that west-frame ocean was falsely classified as land is **rejected**: actual USGS values confirm ocean there. Preserve that attempted hypothesis and the raw counterevidence.

Water level is a separate model. Original ocean water keeps the existing 1 m estimate with tide/datum unknown. Supported inland NHD bodies use the median retained USGS interior samples, with count/spread recorded. The 3,518 bodies without supporting samples remain unresolved. Raw NHD `ELEVATION` values are retained but their units are not assumed to be metres. Material altitude bands are an artistic estimate, not surveyed vegetation, snow or glacier boundaries.

`seattle-regional-manifest.json` pins the numeric source and per-scene typed `DMCCACHE` assets. Only the typed cache is loaded for rendering; the full numeric source is separately auditable and is not parsed again on the critical path. Metadata pins the exact source/context/native inputs, producer and current consumer kernels. All coordinates, normals, colours and indices use explicit typed blocks. Derived render LOD does not discard raw samples. Source-sample residual is bounded to 0.0005 radians in the stated 1,000 m horizontal eye domain **before** collinear boundary recovery; this is not an all-triangle-interior error bound. Actual emitted near-frame edge knots are retained separately.

The published regional transport has `DMCXOR02` magic and the same 16-byte version/JSON-length framing. Each original Float32/Uint32 block uses axis-wise previous-word XOR and component/byte-plane transposition. `regional-array-codec.js` restores every numeric bit and original index order before calling the existing `DMCCACHE` decoder. Transport provenance retains the original compiler/input/kernel pins and numeric-block SHA; only the loader/transport signature is updated. Full-array parity is independently checked in JavaScript against both original caches. No coordinate precision or source geometry is reduced; existing city-context transport is unchanged.

`source-geographic-frame.js` uses WGS84 ECEF/ENU outside a smooth 6–12 km transition. Inside 6 km, the native equirectangular coordinate/height is exact. Numeric heights are used without an invented vertical-datum conversion. The render projection then uses `P'=kP+(1-k)E`, with `k=1` through 6 km and a monotone C1 radial tail tending to 12 km. Original LL/elevations remain unchanged. Standard relative morph targets give main and normal-override passes identical geometry, and outward Float32 bounds cover the declared eye domain. Shared fog reconstructs physical source distance and raw atmospheric elevation; shared weather/preset values are not inflated. The renderer uses a finite source-derived far bound.

The regional geometry is render-only (`noFloor`, no new collision/support/graph). Existing walking/terrain queries remain authoritative. Far water/terrain share clipped boundaries; shoreline side faces connect the retained interpolated terrain to its explicitly estimated water level. Five actual camera clip halfspaces select whole triangle chunks, omitting only the raw-source far plane because its display position is compressed. Every original cache triangle remains available; no tree, crown, source record or preset is thinned for budget. Regional materials participate in the existing season/weather ground-material hook.

Far NHD **render boundaries** have a separate continuous-error LOD. Raw numeric feature/ring/coordinate bits remain lossless. Original OSM and the complete 12 km collar are exact. Outside that collar, source-edge distance bands use a 1,000 m horizontal eye domain, 20–90° effective vertical FOV (including the public 20° photo zoom), physical image height at most 2,160 pixels and aspect at most 2.4. Every original point of each replaced chain is checked against its chord; convexity bounds all original straight-segment interiors and endpoint continuity bounds the reverse Hausdorff direction. A conservative WGS84 curvature term limits/subdivides rendered chord spans. Three original non-collinear anchors retain small rings. Ordered component/hole topology must remain valid or the complete feature reverts. The geometric budget is 0.4 pixel, reserving 0.1 pixel for the measured serialization/projection bound before a 0.5 pixel claim; this is **additional shoreline LOD error**, not a combined DEM/whole-scene error claim. Actual camera conditions outside the envelope are recorded explicitly. The stopped exact-boundary bakes and their costs remain negative evidence; no further source acquisition or detail tiers belong to this candidate.

The candidate is **not native acceptance**. Preserve the rejected 00c images, cold loads and pass budgets. A new immutable combined-source run must check normal walking/Gum Wall, the actual Needle ride and outward elevated circuit, drone-domain bounds, main/Ultra total passes, conditions, cold loading, phone controls and every full-size image. All affected overview pixels must be recaptured from that source before they are current. Pike's facade/canopy/Gum Wall and Seattle Center activity quality remain priority work after this bounded regional test.

Source classification caveat: NHD FTYPE 312 denotes bay/inlet and alone does not prove marine 1 m height. The retained model table's mean-sea candidate labels are unqualified estimates. The reviewed Portage Bay candidates are inside the old excluded frame; Union Bay/Reach are covered by the larger Lake Washington model and protected OSM frame. Retain the negative classification finding and all unresolved level/datum evidence; do not claim surveyed marine grades from that class.
