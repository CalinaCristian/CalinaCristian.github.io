## What the shared mechanics really do

- **Boats:** `game/src/spots/buc.js:515–532` advances a camera/boat automatically by elapsed time, disables walking, allows mouse look and Esc exit, then awards completion only at the end. There is no steering or rowing. Herăstrău’s source path is only **51.4 m / 23.4 s**, not a lake circuit (`buc-herastrau.js:231–235`). Cișmigiu’s filtered path is empty (`cismigiu.js:111–116`); a browser check with both wall time and simulated time in July confirmed the no-op.
- **Narrated visits:** `buc.js:536–549` emits one toast every six seconds while the player stays near the door. Athenaeum and Spring Palace do not enter rooms. This is different from the optional outdoor flag-guide tour, and different again from the instant Parliament/Arc viewpoint transitions.
- **Passport:** the selection takes the first eight named porches with different regex-derived names, not the closest eight (`buc-herastrau.js:173–176`). One target is simply “Stână”; the completion text’s “five regions” is not rigorously established by that algorithm. The shortest geometric five-target candidate includes a leg for which `_navPath` returns null. The 315 m sum of its other legs is **not a complete usable route**. Interaction labels should say “porch” rather than promise an interior.
- **Time/season:** the renderer supports selected visit date and vegetation season (`spots/index.js:331`, `render.js:783–784,873`). Both boat availability checks use **wall-clock `new Date().getMonth()`**, and Cișmigiu’s winter mesh is chosen from wall time at build (`cismigiu.js:24,116`; `buc-herastrau.js:235`). Choosing summer or a July simulated date on 8 November therefore still closes the boats. Arc uses `r3d.simDate` (`arcul-de-triumf.js:83`) but its weekend/holiday rule is a compromise between conflicting sources, not verified current opening hours (`research/activities.md:181–186`). Use one explicit visit date, evaluated in Bucharest time, and state availability; do not silently invent a schedule.
