Course shape is gameplay, not a claim about a real rental route or operator.
- The old April–October check used the browser's current month. It was unverified and is removed. Winter selection disables the warm-season game course; other selected seasons do not imply that a real operator is open.
- Bike participation now means actual pedalling for at least 30 m and an explicit dismount. Animated wheels and pedals show motion. Leave / Escape / mode changes cancel, preserving the last actual dry pose. Returning to a distant station instantly is removed. Source solids, pedestrians and obstacles are retained.
