These are **unimplemented proposals**, one branch per featured scene. Keep all real buildings, source POIs, coordinates, research and neighbouring context; shorten the chosen arrival/route, never shrink geography or move real attractions. The scene owner can change the listed scene file. Shared interactions, input, achievement registration, menu grouping and renderer changes go through their owners; coordinate them before edits. Do not change `READY` until a new review passes.

Every brief targets first interesting input/view in <20 s and an optional **2–5 minute** loop at ordinary walking speed. These are design acceptance targets, not measured completion times. Plaques and tours stay optional, outside the core reward path. Recheck route collisions, action reliability, references, day/night/weather, ≤1M triangles and <3 s load; no software-GL FPS promises.
