# Source parking layout contract

Optional `game/research/parking-layouts-<city>-<district>.json` contains `lots`, keyed by exact `w`/`r` OSM area ID. A record has axial compass `rowBearingDeg`, `stallBearingDeg`, optional independently measured `alternateStallBearingDeg`, confidence, and evidence with official aerial `sourceUrl`, at least two row and three stall pixel line samples (three for each alternate family). Only confidence ≥0.75 applies. Mixed, low quality, parallel and unsupported layout records retain the existing estimate. Explicit OSM orientation/layout always wins; compatible categories permit measured absolute bearings. No lot is created, moved or resized.

Builder sidecars are `areaSourceIds` (parallel to `areas`), `parkingFootprints` (native projected source outer/positive inner rings, without legacy simplification), `parkingLayouts` (accepted records by area index), and `parkingLayoutAudit` (accepted/rejected reason). World areas retain identity through sorting. Row bearings orient rows; stall bearings orient vehicles. Diagonal stall pitch maintains 2.6 m perpendicular separation. Alternate families require their own evidence. Individual spaces, occupancy, aisle spacing and front/rear facing remain explicit runtime estimates.

The complete vehicle body, with 0.1 m margin, must fit the exact lot, avoid all source holes, raised sidewalk/corner bands and ground-intersecting source building bodies. A mapped clear underside or full courtyard is retained. Valid unmeasured candidates consume the same seed and paint sequence; rejected bodies do not shuffle later candidates. `world.stats.parkingClearance` reports candidates, retained, boundary, sidewalk and building rejections. Surface-lot collider metadata retains `parkingSourceId` and `parkingAreaIndex`; no inference from a nearby centroid.

`node tools/osm/parking-layout-check.mjs` exercises four actual OSM lots: SeaTac w276643612 and Bellevue w956124492 measured diagonal layouts, plus mixed source lots w647520469 / w345607324 that remain unchanged. It runs the real compiler and checks every native projected lot vertex, priority, uncertain evidence, alternating families, thin body/sidewalk crossings, elevated clearance, courtyards and separated valid footprints. Reference imagery stays outside Git; fixtures and research include numeric measurements and official URLs only.

The production US research is targeted correction evidence, not a claim that every individual parking space is measured. Shared README/schema notes are owned by the parent integration lane.

Open source roofs do not block the parking centre when their actual clear underside exceeds 2.1 m. The full vehicle footprint is still checked against every ground-level body, lot boundary, hole and raised sidewalk. Estimated canopy support footprints are checked separately and counted as `canopySupport` rejections, so clearing the roof volume cannot admit a parked car through a support. The random seed and default orientation remain unchanged.
