# Bellevue source world

Dataset production commit `a9f77967c37fdf004ebd154609ec3b2f6cd013e9`. These records cover the exact configured core and the analytic 600 m ring, using whole intersecting footprints. They are source checks, not a finished visual-fidelity acceptance.

- All 1,188 eligible positive-area source building bodies match identity and topology: 118 core (including one multipolygon body), 1,070 ring (including 13 multipolygon bodies). Maximum boundary discrepancy is 0.00007 m from serialized coordinate precision. Every mapped courtyard remains.
- All 305 physical road rows preserve original node coordinates, identities and tags, including nine restricted physical rows and 62 short source ways. Public driving access is filtered separately. The extended audit additionally verifies 1,704 scenery pieces from 1,696 source ways, every source polyline vertex in order, and all 1,772 eligible visible-ring ways with zero missing ways/vertices. Covered/tunnel ways outside the physical core are explicitly enumerated exclusions; they are not flattened into open ground scenery.
- All 157 compiled POIs match an original OSM element or explicitly documented official locator within 0.08 m of quantized output, including three transit POIs. There is no compiled core fuel POI; nearby-ring fuel canopy geometry remains in the source building survey. No location or business attributes come from Street View.
- The aerial survey measured 1,641 eligible roof footprints and inspected 80 calibration crops. Reliable roof colour is independent of uncertain shape. Esri acquisition date is unknown; shaded roofs, small pitched buildings and complex compound footprints retain stated uncertainty.
- The final source ring retains 7,133 measured crown positions plus 104 OSM trees. Another 6,171 measured crowns are outside the required district scope; 446 duplicate/invalid geometry samples are rejected. No budget thinning applies. Crown height remains a radius-derived estimate.

`building-source-audit.json` contains every source body. `road-source-audit.json` compares core coordinates with raw OSM XML; `road-source-extended-audit.json` also checks scenery and monotonic source order. `poi-source-audit.json` independently reconstructs source POI locations, names and brands. `source-summary.json` records dataset hash, native height provenance and outstanding acceptance gates. Reference imagery remains outside git.

Visual driving, actual controls, full Street View image comparisons, atlas coverage and mirror/traffic budgets are still being completed. Raw retained building-road conflicts must be classified by mapped layer, passage, access and independently measured width; geometry must not be moved or deleted to make those detections disappear.
