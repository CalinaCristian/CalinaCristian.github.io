# Romană source tram underpass model

`game/src/tram-tunnel-grade.js` supplies a pure source-segment grade Map for the shared rail renderer and Traffic. Its dimensions are explicit **MODEL estimates**, not surveyed elevation or measurements from imagery. No data, original source XY/node identity, motor profile/width, actor, mesh or random sequence is changed.

The west-to-east source chains are:

- `w241374323` reversed → `w241374326` reversed → `w243476552` reversed → `w920117610` reversed.
- `w920117611` → `w920117619` → `w920117620`.

West ground joins are literal rail nodes `2927621025` in ground continuation `w40503646`, and `8544126334` in `w920117625`. East joins are `296077845` at the end of ground source `w920117610`, and `8544117714` in ground source `w920117620`; subsequent connected revenue rail stays at the existing unsurveyed zero datum. Other connections there are source `service=yard`, which the source rail runtime excludes. None of the selected rail nodes equals a native motor node. No nearest-road datum or layer/level-to-metres conversion is used.

The two mapped underground stop nodes `8544117695` / `8544117694` share modeled displacement −7.6 m. The floor has a deep core from 15 m inside each source tunnel endpoint to 15 m before the other endpoint, with C1 ramps to the literal zero joins. This explicit estimated transition keeps the actual source ground roads above the raised pantograph while leaving the tagged tunnel endpoints sloped. Peak model grades are 4.801379% / 4.751887%, below the 8% cap. The rail floor has model half-width 2.8 m, top `0.2 + displacement` and thickness 0.35 m. Both source tracks share a modeled compartment: only its union's outer sides may be walls, since independent walls would obstruct the other track.

Only the flat ±30 m station interval per track initially has a roof descriptor: absolute underside −0.3 m, top 0 m. The 120 m total covered model length does **not** claim the full 297.075 / 297.914 m source-tagged tunnel lengths are covered. Roof extent, depth, transitions and portals remain estimated. Actual posed-body clearance is a separate gate, including the literal 5.79 m pantograph geometry plus the 0.24 m actor anchor; the separate motor tunnel's `maxheight=4.5` does not constrain rail clearance.

`controls-source.json` and `controls-native-source.json` pass the C1/source-node/zero-anchor/metric-tag/extra-branch/conflicting-native-node controls. Thirty complete source legs receive a model, including 27 unchanged rendered legs; six other flat source legs bring the complete rendered Romană network to 33 admitted segments. This includes the seven tagged tunnel and two level-only approach legs. Rendering coverage is unchanged: west approach source ways remain retained and unmodeled where the original render runs omit them.

`road-floor-native6cb.json` checks the actual composed shared support quad polygons against every native road ribbon/profile in immutable `constructor-facts.json` (SHA256 `c25b01cc7acf5b1d7cba51b9d1aa234439f431fcb493e297c0683b67d65496cf`, observed source `6cbf82b61de8267f662bf50239c37ccc1764635c`). Of these overlaps, 226 touch an adjacent motor approach's XY footprint; the rail bed remains 0.04–3.89 m below the retained motor asphalt. The minimum separation beneath other overhead roads is 7.575871 m. The native motor road surfaces stay intact above these lower rail-bed strips.

`posed-final-native6cb.json.gz` independently clips actual posed primary mesh indexed triangles against all native road ribbons, the explicit roof polygons and all 636 outer wall segments generated by the shared union-wall helper. Native `Traffic.placeOnLane` uses US's actual emitted wheel/support fitter; the mesh geometry is checked against the historical constructor's exact vertex count and local bounds, including the 5.78999996 m pantograph. The 770 fixtures cover both actual historical actors 489/490 projected to their nearest source, every source vertex and model/roof knot, and intervals no longer than 5 m. There are 751 whole-body poses, zero headroom/wall failures, zero projected body-triangle overlap with the seven adjacent motor tube/approach road polygons, and 19 explicit exclusions beyond a complete modeled source-body span. All loaded module hashes stay unchanged during the run. `posed-summary.json` is the compact result and `posed-integrity.json` pins every retained raw/compressed diagnostic.

The initial 1.7 m floor width proved too narrow for retaining walls: the rigid 36 m mesh reaches 2.636351 m from its source rail on the actual curve, and 28 checked poses exceed 1.7 m. The rejected initial long-ramp proposal and its immutable floor intersection output remain as `source-proposal-initial-ramp.json` / `road-floor-native6cb-initial-ramp.json`. That proposal produced only 4.488985 m floor-to-ground-road separation and was replaced before integration. It is not a passing headroom model.
